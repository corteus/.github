## How we build

### 01 — Pressure-test

Before architecture, we make the assumptions explicit:

- Who is the buyer?
- How painful is the problem?
- What distribution advantage exists?
- What data is available?
- Why should this become a company?
- Where does AI create a real advantage?

### 02 — Validate

We look for the smallest credible experiment that can produce:

**a user · a design partner · a pilot · revenue · or a useful rejection**

### 03 — Build

Once the evidence holds, we commit engineering capacity and build the production path.

That includes the product, infrastructure, evaluation systems, observability, permissions, workflows, and technical operating layer around the AI.

### 04 — Operate

The operator owns the market.

Corteus carries the technical system until the company can recruit and support its own engineering organization.

## Engineering principles

We build AI systems with explicit boundaries.

**Market before architecture**  
A technically elegant answer to the wrong problem is still the wrong company.

**Code owns predictable work**  
Deterministic policy, state, permissions, queues, retries, and controls stay in software.

**Models handle bounded ambiguity**  
AI gets the minimum authority required for a defined contribution.

**Evidence before confidence**  
Tests, traces, evals, sources, and customer behavior matter more than polished internal narratives.

**Humans retain authority**  
Important external and reputational actions require explicit approval.

## Open-source evidence

We publish small engineering artifacts that make AI product claims easier to test.

### [`wrapper-test`](https://github.com/corteus/wrapper-test)

**Will this product survive the next model release?**

A test surface for examining whether an AI product has durable value beyond a thin dependency on a single model provider.

---

### [`model-rot`](https://github.com/corteus/model-rot)

**What changed when the model changed?**

Makes silent behavioral changes across model updates visible and reviewable.

---

### [`eval-audit`](https://github.com/corteus/eval-audit)

**Are you evaluating the work your system actually performs?**

Checks whether an evaluation suite represents the workload and decisions carried by a production AI system.
